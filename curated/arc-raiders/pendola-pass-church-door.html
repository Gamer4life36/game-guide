A secret basement under the church in Old Town on Pendola Pass. Four symbols scratched on the church wall give a button order. Find the four matching hidden buttons around the church and press them in that order. The order and which of the 14 spots are active change every raid.

WhereOld Town church, Almatera Village, southwest Pendola Pass
Changes every raidThe order, and which 4 of 14 spots are active
RewardBasement: weapon crates, grenade tube, ammo, raider bag, med bag
NeedsNo key, no quest. The map unlocks after 25 rounds.

Getting to Old Town and the church

The church is the biggest building in the village. Look for the tall bell tower, the painted front with red, blue and orange shapes, and a collapsed corner on the roof. Inside there are two levels, joined by a spiral staircase, and the roof can be climbed. The locked basement door is inside, at the east end.

Old Town is also the best place on the map for Research items. Other raiders like to wait inside rooms, and Flash Freeze storms hurt you in the open.

Read the order on the wall

Every raid the wall shows 4 symbols: two different triangles, an omega and a cross. The two triangles are not the same symbol, so check the mark inside each one:

Triangle with a 7A hooked "7" inside, one side dotted. Spots 1, 5, 13.
Triangle with an xA small x inside. Spots 3, 4, 14.
OmegaA horseshoe with a dot on top. Spots 7, 8 (variant), 9.
CrossAn X with arrow marks on both sides. Spots 2, 6, 8, 12.
The symbols by the buttons are often drawn rotated (a triangle may point sideways). Match the mark inside, not the angle. Spots 10 and 11 are triangles, but the photos don't show which one, so check the inner mark in game.

Map of all 14 spots

Click a number to jump to that spot. North is up, and the dashed ring is the church. Spots 2 to 8 are on or inside the church itself.

Only 4 spots are active each raid, one per symbol on the wall. Fastest route: check the church first (spots 2 to 8 cover all four symbols), then circle the houses: 9, then 1 (north), 14, 13, 12 (east and south), 11, then 10 (southwest).

Press the buttons and open the basement

  1. Find the 4 active buttons first, without pressing them, so you don't press one out of order.
  2. Press them in the wall's order, left to right.
  3. A red light means a wrong press. Start again from the first symbol.
  4. After the 4th correct press, go back to the church. The basement door at the east end is open.
  5. Loot: usually most of Weapon Crate (sometimes two), Grenade Tube, Ammo Box, Raider Bag, Medical Bag. Contents are random, and there's no guaranteed rare weapon or blueprint.

Guides disagree on the lights. GamesHorizon says the four lights above the door turn yellow as you go. ShowGamer says each switch has a light: yellow means not pressed, off means pressed correctly, red means wrong. Red always means start over.

Tips

  • No prompt? Several buttons are half-buried. Crouch, jump, or step sideways until "Interact" shows (spots 3 and 12 especially).
  • Stacked rocks (little stone towers) often sit right next to a button.
  • The button is not always under its symbol. It can be on another floor of the same building, in an attic, or around a corner.
  • The church door is not one of the frozen doors, so you don't need a flamethrower.
  • The Clean Hands quest also visits the church tower (Equatorial Sundial), but the puzzle doesn't need it.
  • New ARC on this map: Bully and Skulker. Extract by Ballistic Gondola (up to 3 players).
