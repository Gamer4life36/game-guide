A secret room in the church in Old Town (Almatera Village) on Pendola Pass. Press four hidden buttons in the order shown by four symbols on the church wall. The order and the button spots change every raid, so read the wall each time. No key is needed.

How it works

  1. Get to Old Town. Southwest Pendola Pass, next to the Fallen Emperor area. The map unlocks after 25 rounds played.
  2. Read the wall. Four symbols are scratched low on the church's outside wall, by the snow and broken planks. They are always two triangles (▲), one cross (✚) and one omega (Ω), in a different order each raid.
  3. Find the active buttons. 14 spots are known (below). Each raid, 4 are active, and each active button has its symbol painted nearby. The button can be on another floor or behind debris, and stacked rocks often mark one.
  4. Press them in the wall's order. A wrong press shows red, so start the sequence again.
  5. Loot the basement. The locked door inside the church opens. Expect most of: Weapon Crate (sometimes two), Grenade Tube, Ammo Box, Raider Bag, Medical Bag. Contents are random; one guide calls the loot nothing exceptional.

Symbol reference: every button spot

▲ Triangle (8) · ✚ Cross (4) · Ω Omega (3). Find a symbol, then follow the hint to its button. Click any photo to enlarge it.

▲ Triangle · 8 possible spots

Two triangles are on the wall each raid, so expect 2 of these to be active.

1
Green house near the church

The symbol is on the outside of the house. Button: 2nd floor, between the cupboard and the piano.

3
Church, 2nd floor, by the broken wall

The button is not across the gap. Face northwest and find the rock pile on the same floor. It's behind the rocks; jump if the prompt won't show.

4
Church ground floor, south inside wall

Button: under the spiral staircase, on the left.

5
Church 2nd floor, mural wall

Button: left of the symbol, behind a rock pile next to a circle of pebbles.

10
3-story house southwest of the church

The symbol is outside the attic. You can see it from the church roof or from the ground by the doorway. Button: in the attic, right of the window facing the church, or behind the boxes and covered furniture.

11
White building directly south of the church

Breach the door. Button: ground floor, behind the couch straight ahead.

13
3-story house east of the church

Button: in the attic, under the pile covered with a white tarp.

14
Big wooden crate, east side of the church (outdoors)

Button: in the pile of boxes left of the crate.

✚ Cross · 4 possible spots

One cross is on the wall each raid.

2
Church outer wall, high up, right of the destroyed section

Climb onto the church roof. Button: to the left of the symbol.

6
Inside the church, by the locked door

Face the locked entrance. Button: in the rubble to the right, against the wall.

8
Above the church's west entrance (cross version)

Button: behind the stone pile directly under the cross.

12
Red building southeast of the church

The symbol is on the outside. Button: 2nd floor, left of the southwest window with sheet metal, buried in snow. Crouch repeatedly until the prompt shows.

Ω Omega · 3 possible spots

One omega is on the wall each raid.

7
Bell tower, outside, on top of the church

Button: on the ledge right above the symbol, or below it under the satellite dish.

8
West entrance doorway (omega version)

The doorway shows the omega instead of the cross. Button: around the corner of the wall left of the entrance, behind a stone pile.

9
Yellow house across from the statue, west of the church

The symbol is on the outside. Button: 2nd floor, under a pile of boxes.

Notes

  • Lights: GamesHorizon says the four lights above the door turn yellow as you press the right buttons. ShowGamer says each switch has a light: yellow means not pressed, off means pressed correctly, red means wrong order. Either way, red means start over.
  • Spot 12 is buried in snow, so crouch repeatedly until the prompt shows (GameRant thinks this may be a bug).
  • The church door is not one of the frozen doors, so you don't need a flamethrower.
  • Spot 6's photos have "button-3" in GameRant's file names. If a photo pair looks wrong, compare spots 3 and 6.
  • The puzzle doesn't need the Clean Hands quest, even though that quest also visits the church tower.

Old Town tips

  • Best spot on the map for Research items. Small houses that are quick to loot, with lots of cover, but watch for players waiting in rooms.
  • Flash Freeze storms hurt you in the open, so get under cover. Some other doors freeze shut and need a flamethrower to thaw.
  • New ARC on this map: Bully and Skulker. Extract by Ballistic Gondola (up to 3 players).
